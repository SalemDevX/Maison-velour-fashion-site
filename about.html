Our Story

Born from a desire to tell African stories through fabric, Maison Vélour stands at the intersection of heritage and the future.

2011

The Founding

Adaeze Okonkwo left her position as head designer at a London fashion house with one conviction: African luxury deserved its own language. She opened the first Maison Vélour atelier in a small studio on Victoria Island with three seamstresses and a vision that would change Nigerian fashion forever.

2011
Victoria Island, Lagos
2016

Going Global

After winning the African Fashion International Award in 2014 and 2015, Maison Vélour made its international debut at London Fashion Week. The "Harmattan" collection drew standing ovations and was covered by Vogue, WWD, and The Guardian — placing Nigerian couture on the world map.

2016
London Fashion Week
2025

Today & Beyond

With six ateliers across Lagos, Abuja, Accra, London, New York and Paris, and over 3,200 clients worldwide, Maison Vélour is the definitive voice of contemporary African luxury. We remain committed to ethical sourcing, local artisan partnerships, and telling stories that transcend borders.

2025

Leadership

Board of Trustees

The visionaries who guide Maison Vélour's creative direction, business strategy, and social commitments.

AO
Adaeze Okonkwo
Founder & Creative Director

A graduate of Central Saint Martins, Adaeze founded Maison Vélour in 2011 after a decade designing for European couture houses. Her vision bridges Igbo textile heritage with international luxury.

CE
Chukwuemeka Eze
Chairman, Board of Trustees

Former Director-General of the Nigerian Export Promotion Council, Emeka brings two decades of trade and commerce expertise, steering Maison Vélour's international expansion strategy.

FB
Fatimah Balogun
Chief Financial Officer

A Harvard Business School alumna and former VP at Stanbic IBTC, Fatimah oversees all financial operations, investment portfolios, and sustainable business practices across all six ateliers.

TA
Tunde Adeyemi
Head of Atelier Operations

With 20 years of master-tailoring experience and training under Dior in Paris, Tunde leads the technical excellence of all Maison Vélour workshops, ensuring every stitch meets couture standards.

NO
Ngozi Obi-Achike
Director of Brand & Communications

A former editor at Arise Magazine and brand consultant for Pan-African luxury labels, Ngozi shapes Maison Vélour's voice, visual identity, and media presence across all global markets.

KM
Kwame Mensah
Trustee — Ethical Sourcing

A Ghanaian textile academic and fair-trade advocate, Kwame chairs the Maison Vélour Ethical Sourcing Committee, building relationships with sustainable fabric producers across West Africa.

ZI
Zainab Ibrahim
Lead Textile Designer

Trained at Ravensbourne University London, Zainab leads textile innovation at Maison Vélour — fusing traditional adire and aso-oke techniques with sustainable modern materials.

DP
Dr. David Peters
Trustee — Legal & Governance

A senior partner at Banwo & Ighodalo with a specialism in intellectual property and luxury brand law, David protects the Maison Vélour brand across all 14 countries of operation.
