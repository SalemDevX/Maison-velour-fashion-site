Our Philosophy

The Art of Deliberate Dressing

Founded in 2011 in Lagos, Maison Vélour was born from a conviction that African luxury deserves its own stage. We draw from Yoruba, Igbo, and Efik textile traditions — reimagining heritage for the modern wardrobe.

Every thread is intentional. Every silhouette is considered. Every garment is a statement of quiet, unshakeable authority.
